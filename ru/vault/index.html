Embedded AI

AI risk detection - on your device, even offline

Every signature is analyzed locally by three independent layers. Zero cloud, zero telemetry: unlike cloud transaction simulators, what you sign never leaves your device.

Rules decide

A finite, auditable set of deterministic rules blocks the dangerous shapes: unlimited approvals, collection-wide grants, fake tokens, phishing origins. Only these rules can lock signing - the AI never gates, so nothing can talk it into unlocking either.

Local AI flags

A machine-learning classifier, built on known drainer patterns, scores every request on your device in milliseconds - compact enough to run inside the offline Vault too. It catches the unusual combinations no single rule covers, like 85% of your balance leaving for a first-seen address.

The Vault re-checks

The QR never carries a verdict. The air-gapped Vault re-decodes and re-scores the raw payload with its own embedded copy of the model - a compromised computer gets contradicted on the trusted screen.

Why Locker Exists

Every year, billions of dollars in crypto assets are stolen - not because blockchains are insecure, but because the devices used to sign transactions are compromised. Malware silently alters transaction payloads before they reach your hardware wallet, and by the time you confirm, it's already too late.

Existing solutions all share the same critical flaw: they require a direct connection (USB, Bluetooth, or software bridge) between the signing device and an internet-connected machine. That connection is the attack surface.

Locker was built from the ground up to eliminate that single point of failure. By physically isolating the keystore via a true Air-Gap and verifying exact transaction payloads on a completely offline device, it ensures that even on a fully compromised computer, attackers cannot alter or intercept your signatures.

The smart contracts are 100% open-source. Clone them, audit them, deploy them yourself. No trust required - only math.

Beyond classic time-based locking, Locker Protocol integrates with Uniswap V2/V3 on-chain oracles. You can create a conditional lock: your tokens only unlock when the market price reaches a defined target. The smart contract checks liquidity pools in real time.

When creating the wallet, a 12 or 24-word recovery phrase is provided. Write it on paper and store it safely. If your device is lost or destroyed, install the Vault on a new offline device and import your phrase - all your keys are restored.

The interface is just a façade to interact with the blockchain. It holds no funds and no keys. All code is open-source on GitHub: download it, run it locally, and regain full access to your Locker. Your funds are on the blockchain, not with us.
