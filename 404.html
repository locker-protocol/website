AI risk detection - on your device, even offline

Every signature is analyzed locally by three independent layers. Zero cloud, zero telemetry: unlike cloud transaction simulators, what you sign never leaves your device.

Rules decide

A finite, auditable set of deterministic rules blocks the dangerous shapes: unlimited approvals, collection-wide grants, fake tokens, phishing origins. Only these rules can lock signing - the AI never gates, so nothing can talk it into unlocking either.

Local AI flags

A machine-learning classifier, built on known drainer patterns, scores every request on your device in milliseconds - compact enough to run inside the offline Vault too. It catches the unusual combinations no single rule covers, like 85% of your balance leaving for a first-seen address.

The Vault re-checks

The QR never carries a verdict. The air-gapped Vault re-decodes and re-scores the raw payload with its own embedded copy of the model - a compromised computer gets contradicted on the trusted screen.
